# MaKaDiMi 1.1.7

## New
- **DJ MaKa**: DJ button with an animated orb on Home. Start a session with your music and the DJ greets you and introduces tracks by voice.
- **Search and A–Z sort** for library playlists, so they are easier to find.

## Improved
- **Spotify playlist import** is more complete: if Spotify cuts the read short, the app retries to fetch every track.
- The desktop dock now has the DJ orb in the center.
