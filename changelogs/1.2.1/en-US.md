# MaKaDiMi 1.2.1

## New
- **Natural voice with Deepgram**: the DJ's main voice is now Deepgram (Settings > AI > "DJ natural voice: Deepgram key"). Create a free account at console.deepgram.com, no card needed, with $200 of credit that does not expire and covers thousands of comments. Gemini stays as an optional backup and, if no natural voice responds, the DJ uses the phone voice.
- **Choose how often the DJ speaks** in Settings > AI > "DJ: how often it speaks" (1 to 20).

## Improved
- Gemini's free quota is looked after: requests are spaced, it waits when Google asks and, if the daily quota runs out, it keeps going with the phone voice until it renews, without wasting time or repeating warnings.
