# MaKaDiMi 1.1.9

## New
- **Video quality inside Now Playing**: tap the video and a settings button appears next to the subtitles toggle to pick 1080p, 720p or 360p. It applies instantly and keeps your position. Also available in the fullscreen player.
- **DJ MaKa knows your taste**: when a session starts, the DJ knows your favorite artists and songs and makes personal remarks without repeating itself.

## Improved
- **The DJ only plays when you turn it on** with the DJ button. It is always off when the app opens and goes silent the moment you turn it off. The Settings and quick-panel switches were removed so it can't be enabled by accident.
- **Better DJ voice**: on Android it uses the best installed voice (no internet needed), speaks sentence by sentence with pauses. On Windows it tries the modern system voices first and falls back to the classic one. The DJ text is written to be spoken.
- The desktop DJ now also greets you when the session starts.

## Changed
- **Video subtitles now start off** (toggle them with their button).
