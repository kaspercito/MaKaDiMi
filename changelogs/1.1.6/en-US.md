# MaKaDiMi 1.1.6

## New
- **AI DJ on mobile**: a voice introduces songs between tracks using your phone's voice.
- **"Video & quality" panel** in the player: toggle video mode, change video and audio quality and apply it instantly.
- **Settings with search and categories**: jump straight to any section.

## Improved
- Song menu with quick action cards (download, playlist, radio, share).
- Redesigned bottom navigation bar: floating dock with glow, animations and haptics.
